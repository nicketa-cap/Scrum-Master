# 🧠 Scrum Master AI Agent – Sprint Capacity & Leave Optimizer

---

## 🎯 Objective
Improve sprint planning by balancing leave distribution, reducing dependency risks, and ensuring accurate team capacity using AI-assisted analysis.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement
- Unstructured leave planning  
- Heavy dependency on individuals  
- Sprint delays due to unavailability  
- No visibility into team capacity  

---

## 🤖 Solution Overview
This AI Agent helps Scrum Masters:
- Track leave & availability  
- Identify dependency risks  
- Predict sprint capacity  
- Suggest optimized leave planning  

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

### Step 1: Input Data
- Team members & roles  
- Leave plans  
- Sprint tasks  
- Dependencies  

---

### Step 2: AI Analysis
- Detect leave conflicts  
- Identify capacity gaps  
- Highlight single points of failure  

---

### Step 3: Recommendations
- Balanced leave planning  
- Backup resource suggestions  
- Improved sprint allocation  

---

## 🧠 Decision Logic

- Leave Conflict → 2+ team members on leave on the same day = High Risk  
- Capacity Gap → Allocated work > available hours = Overload  
- Dependency Risk → Task without backup resource = High Risk  

---

## 🧪 Example Scenario

**Input:**
- John and Neha on leave on same day  
- Neha allocated 45 hrs but available 40 hrs  
- API task owned by John with no backup  

**Output:**
- Leave conflict detected  
- Capacity overload identified  
- Dependency risk flagged
